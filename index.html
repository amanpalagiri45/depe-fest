Rules & Regulations

All participants must follow the below rules to ensure a smooth and fair fest experience.

✅

Eligibility & Registration

  • Only registered students with valid college ID are eligible.
  • Registration is valid only after successful payment confirmation.
  • Each participant must register before the final event deadline.
⏰

Punctuality

  • Participants must arrive at least 15 minutes before the event starts.
  • Late entry may not be allowed without prior notice from the coordinator.
  • Team members should stay present for the complete event schedule.
🤝

Code of Conduct

  • Respect all organizers, judges, volunteers, and fellow participants.
  • Any form of cheating, misconduct, or abusive behavior is strictly prohibited.
  • Participants must follow staff instructions at all times.
🎒

Equipment & Safety

  • Bring required personal devices or accessories only if mentioned in the event brief.
  • Do not damage college property, lab equipment, or shared facilities.
  • Follow all safety instructions during workshops, labs, and gaming zones.
💸

Payments & Refunds

  • Entry fees are non-refundable once the registration is confirmed.
  • Any change in team composition must be approved by the event coordinator.
  • Final results and decisions by judges are considered binding.
